How signing in works

Yalendar never asks for your NetID or password, and has no field to type them into. There is no Yale login form anywhere in this app, and nothing is ever sent to a server of mine.

  1. A real Chrome window opens on your computer, pointed at Yale's own CAS page at secure.its.yale.edu.
  2. You type your NetID into Yale's page and approve Duo on your phone, exactly as you would on any other day.
  3. Yale hands that window a session cookie. It is saved to data/session_state.json on your own disk, readable only by you, and git and Docker are both told to exclude it.
